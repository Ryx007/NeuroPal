2. Pairs from a crystal

Pump a nonlinear crystal with a strong blue laser and, very rarely, one pump photon splits into two red ones. The process is called spontaneous parametric down-conversion, and it happens to roughly one pump photon in a million.

2.1 What the split conserves

Energy and momentum are conserved in the split, so the two photons leave correlated in time, in direction and in wavelength. If one goes up, the other goes down; if one is a little bluer, the other is a little redder.

2.2 Heralding

Because the pair is born together, detecting one photon heralds the other. That simple fact turns a weak, random source into a tool: you know when a photon is coming, and roughly where it is going.
